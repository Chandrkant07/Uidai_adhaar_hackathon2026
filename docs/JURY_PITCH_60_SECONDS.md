# 60-second Jury Pitch (UIDAI Leadership)

Chairperson, members of the jury — AadhaarPulse turns UIDAI’s own enrolment and update data into a national early-warning and capacity planning engine.

Every enrolment and every demographic or biometric update is a **signal of service demand**: migration triggers address and mobile updates, youth transitions trigger biometric refresh, and operational friction shows up as abnormal spikes, repeated corrections, and regional backlogs.

AadhaarPulse unifies the three official datasets into a district-month panel, detects anomalies, and forecasts the next 6 to 12 months of enrolment and update load.

At the centre is our decision metric — the **Aadhaar Service Stress Index, ASSI** — combining enrolment growth, update volumes, and biometric refresh pressure to rank districts by operational stress.

With ASSI and our what-if simulator, UIDAI can answer: where should we add centres, where should we run biometric refresh drives, and which districts need outreach to reduce repeated corrections.

This is actionable governance intelligence — better SLAs, targeted inclusion, and smarter resource allocation — using only UIDAI’s official aggregated data.
